Vision

To impart quality technical education through research, innovation, and teamwork for creating professionally superior and ethically strong manpower that meets the global challanges of engineering industries and reasearch organisations.

Mission

  • Enable graduates to become digital age literates, innovators, and professionals.

  • Prepare graduates for lifelong learning and career challenges.

Program Specific Outcomes

  2. Adapt to emerging technological advancements including cybersecurity, AR/VR, and IoT.
